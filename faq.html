Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about Private Talk

Getting Started

What is Private Talk?

Private Talk is a secure, browser-based messaging platform designed for private conversations. It allows you to chat with others without requiring account registration or sharing personal information.

How do I start using Private Talk?

Simply visit our website and you can start chatting immediately. No downloads, installations, or account creation required. Just open your browser and begin your private conversation.

Is Private Talk free to use?

Yes! Private Talk is completely free to use. We believe that privacy should be accessible to everyone, regardless of their financial situation.

What devices can I use Private Talk on?

Private Talk works on any device with a modern web browser, including:

  • Desktop computers (Windows, Mac, Linux)
  • Laptops
  • Tablets (iPad, Android tablets)
  • Smartphones (iPhone, Android phones)

Privacy & Security

How does Private Talk protect my privacy?

Private Talk is designed with privacy as its core principle. We don't require personal information to use the service, we don't store your messages on our servers, and all communication is processed locally on your device whenever possible.

Are my messages encrypted?

Yes, Private Talk uses secure communication protocols to protect your messages during transmission. Our browser-based approach also means that message processing happens on your device, adding an additional layer of privacy.

Does Private Talk store my messages?

No, we don't store your messages on our servers. Your conversations exist only during the active session and on the devices of the participants. Once you close the session, the messages are gone.

Can anyone else read my conversations?

Your conversations are private between you and your chat partners. We don't have access to the content of your messages, and they're not stored or logged on our servers.

Features & Usage

How do I invite someone to chat?

You can invite others to chat by sharing a unique link or room code. Simply create a new conversation and share the provided link with anyone you want to chat with.

Can I send files or images?

Private Talk currently focuses on text-based messaging to maintain simplicity and security. We may add additional features in the future based on user feedback.

Is there a limit to how many people can be in a conversation?

Private Talk supports both one-on-one conversations and group chats. The exact limit depends on the type of conversation and server capacity, but most use cases are fully supported.

What happens if I lose my internet connection?

If you lose your connection, you'll be temporarily disconnected from the chat. Once your connection is restored, you should be able to rejoin. However, since we don't store messages, any messages sent while you were offline may not be delivered to you.

Technical Questions

Why use a browser-based messaging app?

Browser-based apps offer several advantages:

  • No installation required - instant access from any device
  • Always up-to-date - you automatically get the latest version
  • Cross-platform compatibility - works on any operating system
  • No app store permissions - more control over your device
What browsers are supported?

Private Talk works best on modern browsers including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. We recommend keeping your browser updated to the latest version for the best experience and security.

Do I need to enable any special browser settings?

Private Talk works with standard browser settings. However, you'll need to ensure that JavaScript is enabled (which is the default for most browsers). Some ad blockers may need to be configured to allow our site.
